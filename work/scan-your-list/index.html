A hint of magic

Working with an LLM in the background for the first time resulted in needing to handle an extra long loading time. Our normal skeleton loading state was not going to be enough. I designed and built a Lottie animation: light movement to keep you interested, a hint of magic, and language describing what we are doing behind the scenes for transparency.

Personalized matches with the option to browse for more

I proposed an approach that balances speed and user control by prioritizing a personalized match with the option to browse other options. In our testing, we found that people often had multiple Buy it again product matches, and we did not always pick the right one because our systems are constrained to recommend based on past behavior and can’t predict future events one has in their life.

I compiled a dataset of images to test LLM behavior

I’ve been leaning into finding opportunities to help shape backend behavior by injecting customer centric insights. Building a dataset of realistic shopping list images enabled our team to be grounded in real scenarios and discover use cases that needed to be handled.

I got to branch off of a POC that my iOS engineer started, build out multiple design explorations, and use them in my own life when planning my groceries.
