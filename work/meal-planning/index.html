It started as a chatbot

Our Data Science team built a proof of concept called RecipeBot. You could chat with an H-E-B assistant, ask it for a recipe, and it answered with ingredients and instructions. I volunteered to stay close to the project because I was curious about AI. This was 2024, very much the early days of LLMs.

Guiding people instead of asking them to prompt

I designed a guided experience that helped people make a few key selections, like proteins, ingredients they already have and lifestyle preferences. Each one increased the chance of relevant results, without anyone having to write a prompt. People could then replace or remove any recipe to make the plan their own.

Swapping recipes felt like a game

On the results page, people could replace any recipe, remove the ones they didn’t want, and swipe to the end to add another. Letting people replace recipes made the experience feel exciting, almost like a game, and we found that giving people that level of control increased their delight.

Visualizing the data journey

I worked closely with Data Science as they built the backend workflow, and visualized the data journey so we could share what we were building with stakeholders. Selections from the interface and anything people typed became filters, which narrowed the search space before a semantic search found the closest recipes.

Customers planned their week with it

I worked with Design Technology to build a functional prototype, and we let customers use it for their weekly planning and report back on its value. Here’s what we learned.

  • Customers were excited about the experience, and many felt it would help them quickly narrow down what to make that week.

  • They talked about how it helped them figure out what to make with a set of ingredients they already have.

  • They were looking for more diverse options in their meal plan results.

Outcome

The assistant was sunset, but what we learned lives on in search

The project ended up being sunset. However, its learnings resulted in green-lighting investments in multiple projects that use semantic search and AI to improve our global search experience.
